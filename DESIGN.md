## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for high-end luxury fashion e-commerce where architectural restraint meets intelligent precision. The interface acts as a silent gallery frame: content and garment imagery lead, while interface mechanics remain quiet, razor-sharp, and tactile.

- **Brand Personality**: Discerning, editorial, effortless, technologically astute. The product balances the gallery-level confidence of modern luxury houses (Acne Studios, Totême, Farfetch) with the seamless, whisper-quiet micro-precision of premium consumer tech.
- **Target Audience**: Style-conscious consumers and modern luxury buyers who demand fastidious attention to garment craftsmanship, cut, and fit accuracy, with zero tolerance for visual clutter or intrusive gimmicks.
- **Emotional Response**: Immediate perception of bespoke luxury, total trust in predictive fit metrics, and tactile satisfaction across every point of friction (sizing, checkout, selection).
- **Design Movement**: Elevated Warm Minimalism infused with Tactical Glassmorphism. High-density negative space, sub-pixel border definitions, subdued linen and warm chalk surfaces, and sharp typographic hierarchy, punctuated by high-fidelity glass surfaces for predictive contextual modules like the Fit Assistant.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural monochrome base warmed by organic stone undertones, paired with hyper-refined semantic jewel tones reserved exclusively for AI certainty and fit diagnostics.

- **Primary (`#111110`)**: Deep Carbon. A softened obsidian black avoiding the harshness of pure `#000000`, anchoring headings, primary purchase actions, and crisp borders.
- **Secondary (`#2A5C43`)**: Atelier Emerald. A muted, heritage mineral green reserved for "True to Size" validation, positive fit confidence matches (90%+ fit certainty), and verified measurements.
- **Tertiary (`#3142A5`)**: Precision Indigo. Used for algorithmic cues, active AI state indicators, interactive biometric sizing sliders, and predictive garment drape tags.
- **Neutral (`#767471`)**: Muted Porphyry. A balanced, warm charcoal neutral for supporting details, model measurements, and passive secondary state tags.
- **Surface Roles**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAFAF8` (Warm Alabaster / Chalk).
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White for crisp garment contrast).
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F3F2EE` (Warm Sand tint for technical specs and pill backdrops).
  - `border-hairline`: `rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.08)` (Subtle separation lines).
  - `border-active`: `rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.85)` (High-intent selections).

## Typography

The type system pairs the geometric clarity of Geist with the technical rigour of JetBrains Mono for measurement readouts and fit analytics.

- **Editorial Presence**: Product titles utilize `display-xl` with tight tracking (`-0.03em`), creating an authoritative, modern look reminiscent of printed lookbooks.
- **Numerical & Algorithmic Rigour**: Sizing chips, numeric fit percentages, bust/waist/hip dimensions, and tolerance variances strictly utilize `label-code` via JetBrains Mono. This deliberate contrast introduces computational authority to the Fit Assistant without compromising typographic elegance.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Secondary specs (fabric composition, care, provenance) rely on `body-md` in Carbon or Muted Porphyry. `label-caps` is used for category tracking, micro-eyebrows, and certified badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The Product Detail Page (PDP) uses an asymmetrical split-pane architecture engineered around product imagery and persistent conversion actions.

- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 
  - 12-column layout with a 60/40 or 58/42 split.
  - Left Pane (7-8 columns): Fluid editorial gallery showing staggered lookbook imagery, detailed fabric crops, and dynamic runway clips with `gutter` spacing of `1.5rem`.
  - Right Pane (4-5 columns): Pinned, sticky buying container housing the product metadata, interactive size matrix, 'Fit Assistant' trigger card, and accordion modules.
  - Outer margins remain generous at `3rem` (`margin`) to guarantee high-end breathing room.
- **Mobile / Small Screens (< 1024px)**:
  - Collapses into a single stacked column.
  - Edge-to-edge carousel with full-bleed garment slides and paginated dot/fraction tracking (`margin-mobile` applied to editorial text blocks only).
  - Sizing and primary CTA dock to an ultra-refined sticky bottom action drawer.
- **Component Flow**: Internal component padding follows a strict 4px/8px incremental base rhythm. Fit Assistant sub-cards use `space-md` (`1rem`) internal padding to preserve high density and touch compliance.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows. Depth is achieved through controlled tonal layering, sub-pixel borders, and subtle glass surfaces.

- **Tiers of Elevation**:
  - **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat `#FAFAF8`.
  - **Level 1 (Inlaid Module / Spec Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.08)`). Zero box-shadow.
  - **Level 2 (Fit Assistant Drawer / Floating Pill CTAs)**: Translucent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.03)`.
  - **Level 3 (Modal Overlay / Biometric Sizing Sheets)**: `#FFFFFF` framed by a subtle perimeter scrim (`rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.4)`), accompanied by a diffused elevation shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.14)`.
- **Ghost Borders & Dividing Geometry**:
  - Partitions between sizing grids, fit summaries, and accordion rows use single-pixel divider rules tinted with `rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.06)` to eliminate heavy visual scaffolding.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a Soft Architectural shape language (`roundedness: 1`), projecting restraint, structure, and tailored precision.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Standard size selector chips, secondary input frames, accordion panels, and image container viewports.
- **Moderate Radius (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem / 8px)**: Reserved for composite cards, the 'Smart Stitch Fit' interactive module, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem / 12px)**: Applied exclusively to floating sheets, bottom sheets on mobile, and sizing recommendation popovers.
- **Pill Exception**: Full circular radii (`rounded-full`) are strictly preserved for status tags (e.g., "98% Fit Match", "Only 2 Left", "Low Stock") to maintain distinct visual semantics separate from actionable structural components.

## Components

### 1. Primary Purchase & Action Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Bag")**: Full-width solid Deep Carbon (`#111110`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), 52px height, 4px border radius. Hover: `#222220`. Active state: subtle scale down (`0.99`). Monospace price lockups inset to the right.
- **Secondary CTA / Action Button**: Transparent base with hairline border (`rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.15)`), Carbon text. Hover: `border-color: #111110` with background `#F3F2EE`.

### 2. Size Selector Grid & Availability Matrix
- Segmented rectangular chips with hairline border separation.
- **Default State**: White background, `Geist` body-md, `#111110`.
- **Selected State**: Deep Carbon background, white text.
- **Recommended Fit Highlight**: Highlighted with an Atelier Emerald accent dot (4px) or subtle Emerald border glow (`rgba(42, 92, 67, 0.3)`) indicating AI fit match.
- **Sold Out State**: Muted neutral text with a clean, 45-degree diagonal hairline slash (`rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.15)`).

### 3. Integrated 'Fit Assistant' / Smart Stitch Widget
- **Trigger Component**: Inlaid card directly above the size matrix. White background with a delicate Precision Indigo border hairline (`rgba(49, 66, 165, 0.18)`). Displays an ambient spark icon, prompt label ("Calculate your precision cut"), and quick profile switch.
- **Active Recommendation Pill**: Once calibrated, transforms into a high-confidence readout: "Recommended for you: IT 48 · 94% Match" with an Atelier Emerald indicator tag.
- **Interactive Sizing Sheet / Popover**:
  - Slides from right on desktop (440px width); bottom drawer on mobile.
  - Dual-mode input: Quick Fit (height, weight, body shape profile) and Garment Comparison (matches dimensions against items previously bought).
  - Interactive Tension Sliders: Discrete sliders to toggle between "Skinny / Snug / Tailored / Relaxed / Oversized" fit preferences.
  - Visual Garment Drape Heatmap: Simplified geometric silhouette showing drape points (shoulders, chest, waist, length) with semantic tags (e.g., "Slightly tight at chest, perfect drape at hem").

### 4. Input Fields & Precision Steppers
- Clean 44px height inputs with 4px border radius.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.12)`.
- Active / Focus: Border changes to `#111110` with zero heavy outer ring; JetBrains Mono numeric font inputs for measurements (cm/in).

### 5. Chips, Badges & Verification Tags
- **Confidence Badges**: Pill-shaped, `6px` padding horizontal, `2px` vertical. 
  - Emerald variant: Background `rgba(42, 92, 67, 0.08)`, text `#2A5C43`.
  - Indigo variant: Background `rgba(49, 66, 165, 0.08)`, text `#3142A5`.
- **Model Metric Tags**: Minimalist text chips displaying: "Model is 188cm / 6'2\" wearing size 48 (Loose Fit)".

### 6. Product Detail Accordions
- Border-top hairline only (`rgba(17, 17, 16, 0.08)`).
- 56px touch height, seamless expansion with spring easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- Dedicated tabs: Fabric & Technical Specs, Atelier Measurements, Artisanal Provenance, Shipping & Transparent Returns.
